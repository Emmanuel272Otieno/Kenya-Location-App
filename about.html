🌍 About Kenya Location Tracker

Learn about our project, purpose and goals.

Technology for a Connected Community.

We are developing a platform that helps people voluntarily share their own location with trusted contacts.

🇰🇪
🎯

Our Mission

To make voluntary location sharing simple and accessible while respecting individual privacy.

🔭

Our Vision

A connected community that uses technology responsibly to support communication and safety.

🤝

Our Values

Respect, trust, privacy, responsibility and innovation.

Project Objectives

  • Display a user's own GPS location.
  • Allow users to manage trusted contacts.
  • Support voluntary location sharing.
  • Teach users about privacy and digital safety.
  • Provide an accessible mobile-friendly platform.

How It Helps the Community

The project can help people share their whereabouts with trusted family members and friends when they choose. It can also encourage digital skills and responsible technology use in the community.
