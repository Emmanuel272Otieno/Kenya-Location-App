Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view my location?

Open Tracker, agree to location access and select Start Tracking. Your browser will ask for permission.

Why is my location not showing?

Check your device's location settings and browser permissions. Location access generally requires HTTPS or localhost.

How do I add a trusted contact?

Open Contacts, enter the person's name and phone number, then select Add Contact.

How do I stop tracking?

Open Tracker and select Stop Tracking.

Does the platform secretly track people?

No. The project is designed for people to share their own location voluntarily.
